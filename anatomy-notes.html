Medical study notes for Anatomy

1. Introduction to Anatomy

Anatomy is the study of the structure of the human body and the relationships between its different parts.

Important Areas

  • Gross Anatomy
  • Microscopic Anatomy
  • Developmental Anatomy
  • Clinical Anatomy

2. Anatomical Position

The anatomical position is a standard position used to describe the location and relationship of body structures.

Key Features

  • Body standing upright
  • Face directed forward
  • Arms positioned at the sides
  • Palms facing forward
  • Feet directed forward

3. Anatomical Planes

Anatomical planes are imaginary flat surfaces used to divide the body for description.

Main Planes

  • Sagittal Plane
  • Coronal Plane
  • Transverse Plane

4. Anatomical Directions

Directional terms help describe the location of one body structure in relation to another.

Common Terms

  • Superior and Inferior
  • Anterior and Posterior
  • Medial and Lateral
  • Proximal and Distal
  • Superficial and Deep
