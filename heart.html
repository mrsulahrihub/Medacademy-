❤️ Heart Anatomy

Important anatomy notes for medical students

📖 Introduction

The heart is a muscular organ that pumps blood throughout the body. It is located in the thoracic cavity between the lungs.

📍 Location

  • Located in the thoracic cavity.
  • Situated between the two lungs.
  • Present in the middle mediastinum.
  • The apex is directed downward and to the left.

🏛️ Chambers of the Heart

Chamber Main Role
Right Atrium Receives deoxygenated blood.
Right Ventricle Pumps blood toward the lungs.
Left Atrium Receives oxygenated blood.
Left Ventricle Pumps blood to the body.

🚪 Heart Valves

Valve Location
Tricuspid Valve Right atrium → right ventricle
Pulmonary Valve Right ventricle → pulmonary artery
Mitral Valve Left atrium → left ventricle
Aortic Valve Left ventricle → aorta

🩸 Major Blood Vessels

  • Aorta
  • Superior Vena Cava
  • Inferior Vena Cava
  • Pulmonary Artery
  • Pulmonary Veins

🔄 Blood Flow Through Heart

Body → Superior/Inferior Vena Cava → Right Atrium → Right Ventricle → Pulmonary Artery → Lungs → Pulmonary Veins → Left Atrium → Left Ventricle → Aorta → Body

⚡ Quick Revision

  • Heart has 4 chambers.
  • Right side receives and pumps deoxygenated blood.
  • Left side receives and pumps oxygenated blood.
  • There are 4 main heart valves.
  • Left ventricle pumps blood into the aorta.

🎯 Important Exam Points

  • How many chambers does the heart have? → Four
  • Which chamber pumps blood into the aorta? → Left ventricle
  • Which valve is between the left atrium and left ventricle? → Mitral valve
  • Which valve is between the right atrium and right ventricle? → Tricuspid valve
