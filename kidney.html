🫘 Kidney Anatomy

Basic anatomy of the human kidney

Introduction

The kidneys are paired organs that are located in the posterior part of the abdomen. They play an important role in maintaining fluid and electrolyte balance.

Parts of Kidney

  • Renal Capsule
  • Renal Cortex
  • Renal Medulla
  • Renal Pelvis

Functional Unit

The nephron is the structural and functional unit of the kidney.

Major Functions

  • Formation of urine
  • Removal of waste products
  • Regulation of fluid balance
  • Regulation of electrolytes
