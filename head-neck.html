🧠 Head & Neck Anatomy

Important structures, muscles, vessels and nerves

📚 Introduction

The head and neck contain important structures related to the brain, special senses, respiration, digestion and communication.

Major structures include:

  • Skull and facial bones
  • Brain
  • Face and scalp
  • Neck muscles
  • Major arteries and veins
  • Cranial nerves
  • Pharynx and larynx
  • Thyroid gland

💀 Skull

The skull forms the bony framework of the head and protects the brain.

Major bones of the skull:

  • Frontal bone
  • Parietal bones
  • Temporal bones
  • Occipital bone
  • Sphenoid bone
  • Ethmoid bone

Important facial bones:

  • Maxilla
  • Mandible
  • Zygomatic bone
  • Nasal bones

👤 Face

The muscles of facial expression are mainly supplied by the facial nerve.

Important muscles:

  • Orbicularis oculi
  • Orbicularis oris
  • Buccinator
  • Frontalis
  • Zygomaticus major

These muscles are involved in facial expressions such as smiling, closing the eyes and moving the lips.

💪 Muscles of the Neck

Sternocleidomastoid

The sternocleidomastoid is an important superficial muscle of the neck. It helps with movements of the head and neck.

Trapezius

The trapezius contributes to movements of the shoulder and neck.

Suprahyoid muscles

These muscles are associated with movements of the hyoid bone and swallowing.

Infrahyoid muscles

These muscles help in movements of the hyoid bone and larynx.

🫁 Pharynx

The pharynx is a muscular tube shared by the respiratory and digestive pathways.

Three parts:

  • Nasopharynx
  • Oropharynx
  • Laryngopharynx

🗣️ Larynx

The larynx is an organ of the respiratory tract located in the neck. It is important for voice production and protection of the lower airway.

Important structures:

  • Thyroid cartilage
  • Cricoid cartilage
  • Epiglottis
  • Vocal folds

🦋 Thyroid Gland

The thyroid gland is located in the anterior neck, adjacent to the larynx and upper trachea.

Main parts:

  • Right lobe
  • Left lobe
  • Isthmus

The thyroid produces hormones that have important effects on metabolism and growth.

🩸 Major Arteries

Common Carotid Arteries

The common carotid arteries supply blood to the head and neck through their branches.

Internal Carotid Artery

The internal carotid artery contributes significantly to the blood supply of the brain.

External Carotid Artery

The external carotid artery supplies many structures of the face, scalp and neck.

Vertebral Arteries

The vertebral arteries ascend through the neck and contribute to the blood supply of the brain.

🩸 Major Veins

  • Internal jugular vein
  • External jugular vein
  • Anterior jugular vein

The internal jugular vein is an important venous drainage pathway from the brain, face and neck.

🧠 Cranial Nerves

There are 12 pairs of cranial nerves. They are involved in sensory, motor and autonomic functions of the head and neck, with some also extending beyond the neck.

  1. Olfactory (I)
  2. Optic (II)
  3. Oculomotor (III)
  4. Trochlear (IV)
  5. Trigeminal (V)
  6. Abducens (VI)
  7. Facial (VII)
  8. Vestibulocochlear (VIII)
  9. Glossopharyngeal (IX)
  10. Vagus (X)
  11. Accessory (XI)
  12. Hypoglossal (XII)

For detailed cranial nerve study, visit the dedicated Cranial Nerves notes.

👁️ Special Senses

  • Eye — vision
  • Ear — hearing and balance
  • Nose — smell
  • Tongue — taste

Several cranial nerves are responsible for special sensory functions.

🧠 Trigeminal Nerve (CN V)

The trigeminal nerve is the major sensory nerve of the face and also supplies the muscles of mastication.

Three major divisions:

  • Ophthalmic division (V1)
  • Maxillary division (V2)
  • Mandibular division (V3)

🙂 Facial Nerve (CN VII)

The facial nerve supplies the muscles of facial expression. It also carries taste sensation from part of the tongue and has other functions.

Important exam point:

Facial nerve = major motor nerve of facial expression.

👅 Hypoglossal Nerve (CN XII)

The hypoglossal nerve mainly supplies the muscles involved in movements of the tongue.

Important exam point:

Hypoglossal nerve is the major motor nerve of the tongue.

⭐ Important Exam Points

  • Skull protects the brain.
  • Mandible is the major movable bone of the face.
  • Facial nerve (CN VII) supplies muscles of facial expression.
  • Trigeminal nerve (CN V) is the major sensory nerve of the face.
  • Trigeminal nerve has V1, V2 and V3 divisions.
  • Hypoglossal nerve (CN XII) supplies tongue muscles.
  • Internal carotid artery contributes to brain blood supply.
  • External carotid artery supplies many structures of the face and scalp.
  • Internal jugular vein is an important venous drainage pathway.
  • Pharynx has nasopharynx, oropharynx and laryngopharynx.
  • Larynx is important for voice production and airway protection.
  • Thyroid gland has right and left lobes connected by an isthmus.

⚡ Quick Revision

  • Skull: Protects the brain
  • Face: Facial expression and sensory structures
  • CN V: Trigeminal — facial sensation and mastication
  • CN VII: Facial — facial expression
  • CN XII: Hypoglossal — tongue movement
  • Pharynx: Common pathway for respiratory and digestive systems
  • Larynx: Voice and airway protection
  • Thyroid: Endocrine gland of the neck
  • Carotid arteries: Major arterial supply to head and neck
  • Jugular veins: Major venous drainage of head and neck
