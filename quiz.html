🩺 Most Common Diseases — Causes & Treatment

Common

🌡️ Fever

Causes: Infection, inflammation, viral or bacterial diseases.

Symptoms: Increased body temperature, chills, sweating, weakness.

Management: Fluids, rest and treatment of the underlying cause. Antipyretics may be used when appropriate.

Respiratory

🤧 Common Cold

Cause: Usually viral infection, commonly rhinoviruses.

Symptoms: Runny nose, sneezing, sore throat and mild cough.

Treatment: Rest, fluids and symptomatic treatment. Antibiotics do not treat viral colds.

Respiratory

🦠 Influenza (Flu)

Cause: Influenza viruses.

Symptoms: Fever, body aches, fatigue, headache, cough and sore throat.

Treatment: Rest and fluids; antiviral treatment may be appropriate for selected patients when started early.

Respiratory

😷 Cough

Causes: Viral infection, asthma, allergy, smoking, GERD and other conditions.

Management: Treatment depends on the cause. Persistent or severe cough should be medically evaluated.

Metabolic

🍬 Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus

Cause: Insulin resistance with progressive impairment of insulin secretion.

Symptoms: Polyuria, polydipsia, increased hunger, fatigue and sometimes blurred vision.

Management: Lifestyle modification, glucose monitoring and appropriate medicines such as metformin or other glucose-lowering therapies according to clinical assessment.

Neurology

🤕 Headache

Causes: Tension headache, migraine, dehydration, infection, sleep problems and many other causes.

Management: Depends on the underlying cause. Hydration, rest and appropriate analgesia can help some uncomplicated headaches.

Neurology

🧠 Migraine

Symptoms: Recurrent headache, often throbbing, with nausea and sensitivity to light or sound.

Triggers: Sleep disturbance, stress, certain foods, hormonal changes and other individual triggers.

Treatment: Trigger avoidance, appropriate acute medicines and preventive therapy for frequent attacks.

Renal

🪨 Kidney Stones

Causes: Concentrated urine, calcium oxalate or other crystal formation, dehydration and metabolic factors.

Symptoms: Severe flank pain, hematuria, nausea and urinary symptoms.

Treatment: Depends on stone size and location; fluids and pain control may be used, while some stones require medical procedures.

Renal

🚽 Urinary Tract Infection (UTI)

Cause: Usually bacterial infection of the urinary tract.

Symptoms: Dysuria, urinary frequency, urgency and sometimes fever.

Treatment: Appropriate antibiotic therapy when bacterial infection is confirmed or clinically suspected.

Renal

🫘 Chronic Kidney Disease

Common Causes: Diabetes mellitus and hypertension are major causes.

Symptoms: Early disease may have few symptoms; advanced disease may cause fatigue, edema, nausea and changes in urination.

Management: Control blood pressure and diabetes, monitor kidney function, manage complications and avoid nephrotoxic exposures where possible.

Emergency

⚠️ When to Seek Urgent Medical Care

Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, confusion, seizure, severe dehydration, persistent high fever, blood in urine with severe pain, or sudden weakness/speech difficulty requires urgent medical assessment.
