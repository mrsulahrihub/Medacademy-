🩺 Common Diseases — Quick Review

Common

🌡️ Fever

Causes: Infections, inflammation and other conditions.

Management: Fluids, rest and treatment of the underlying cause.

Respiratory

🤧 Common Cold

Cause: Usually viral infection, commonly rhinoviruses.

Symptoms: Runny nose, sneezing, sore throat and cough.

Metabolic

🍬 Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus

Cause: Insulin resistance with progressive impairment of insulin secretion.

Management: Lifestyle modification, glucose monitoring and appropriate treatment.

Neurology

🧠 Migraine

Symptoms: Recurrent headache, often throbbing, with nausea and sensitivity to light or sound.

Renal

🫘 Kidney Stones

Symptoms: Severe flank pain, hematuria and sometimes nausea or urinary symptoms.

Emergency

⚠️ Emergency Warning

Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, confusion, seizure, sudden weakness or speech difficulty requires urgent medical assessment.
