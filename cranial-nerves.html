🧠 Cranial Nerves

Overview of the twelve cranial nerves

Introduction

There are twelve pairs of cranial nerves. They are identified using Roman numerals from I to XII.

Cranial Nerves I–VI

  1. Olfactory
  2. Optic
  3. Oculomotor
  4. Trochlear
  5. Trigeminal
  6. Abducens

Cranial Nerves VII–XII

  1. Facial
  2. Vestibulocochlear
  3. Glossopharyngeal
  4. Vagus
  5. Accessory
  6. Hypoglossal
